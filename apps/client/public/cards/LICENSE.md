Files are named `<rank><suit>.svg` (rank `2–9 T J Q K A`, suit `s h d c`), e.g. `As.svg`,
`Td.svg`. The game's card ids are the same with an upper-case suit (`AS`, `TD`); see
`src/lib/cardImages.ts`.
