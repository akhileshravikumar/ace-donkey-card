- Players can create or join a game lobby/room.
- Each room supports a maximum of 7 players.
- Players can choose their own display names.
- Shuffle all 52 cards randomly before dealing.
- Distribute cards equally among players, with remaining cards handled according to the game rules.
- Each player can view only their own cards.
- Players can single-click to highlight a card.
- Players can double-click to play a card.
- Players can drag and drop cards to rearrange or play them.
- Provide an auto-sort button to organize cards by suit.
- Players can play only valid cards according to the game rules.
- Highlight the player holding the Ace of Spades as the starting player.
- The starting player can lead with any card.
- Players must follow the suit led whenever possible.
- Players can play any card only when they do not have the required suit.
- Track the highest-value card in every round.
- If a player cuts the suit, the highest card wins the round and takes all cards on the table.
- If all players follow the same suit, fold the cards on the table.
- The player with the highest card starts the next round in either case.
- Award the most points to the first player who finishes all their cards.
- Track player rankings and scores.
- Provide a give-up option for the last two remaining players.
- Declare the last remaining player as the loser.
- Assign the loser the Ace of Spades for the next game.
- Deal the remaining cards equally among the other players.
- Track each player's consecutive losses.
- After 1 consecutive loss, assign the Ace of Spades.
- After 2 consecutive losses, assign the Ace of Clubs.
- After 3 consecutive losses, assign the Ace of Diamonds.
- After 4 consecutive losses, assign the Ace of Hearts.
- Reset a player's losing streak only when they win a game.
- Preserve the game state and player progress when a player temporarily disconnects.
- Synchronize card plays, turns, scores and round results across all players.
