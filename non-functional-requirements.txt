- Performance: Game actions, card plays and score updates should happen with minimal delay.
- Low Latency: Player actions and game-state updates should be reflected across all players with minimal delay.
- Real-Time Synchronization: All players must see identical turns, round results and game outcomes.
- Scalability: Support multiple concurrent rooms without performance degradation.
- Reliability: Prevent game crashes, lost progress and unexpected interruptions.
- Security: Protect player accounts, room access and game data.
- Fairness: Ensure genuinely random card shuffling and prevent cheating.
- Availability: Keep the game accessible with minimal downtime.
- Usability: Provide a simple, intuitive and easy-to-use browser interface.
- Compatibility: Support major browsers across desktop, tablet and mobile devices.
- Recoverability: Allow players to reconnect after network disconnections and resume the ongoing game without losing progress, within a 1-minute timeout.
- Responsiveness: Adapt the interface to different screen sizes and orientations.
- Card Interaction: Support single-click to highlight, double-click to play, drag-and-drop to rearrange or play cards, and an auto-sort option to organize cards by suit.
- Card Visibility: Clearly display each player's remaining card count while keeping their actual cards private.
- Player Status Visibility: Visually mark the previous game's loser as the Joker and clearly display their current losing streak and assigned Ace.
- Scoreboard: Provide a clear and easily accessible score table showing player rankings, points and game results.
- Low Bandwidth Support: Work smoothly with slow or unstable internet connections.
- Data Integrity: Maintain accurate scores, card counts, rankings and losing streaks.
- Privacy: Never expose a player's private cards or personal information to other players.
- Anti-Cheating: Prevent unauthorized moves, card manipulation and exploitation of game logic.
- Maintainability: Keep the code modular and easy to update.
- Auditability: Maintain logs of card plays, game outcomes and player actions.
- Resource Efficiency: Minimize CPU, memory, battery and network consumption.
- Accessibility: Provide readable text, clear cards and accessible controls.
- Game State Consistency: Ensure all players see the same cards on the table, current player, valid moves, scores and game outcomes.
